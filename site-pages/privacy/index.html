Open source

Everything below can be checked in the source code. NOBAD is an independent, free and open-source studio.

01NOBAD VPN (the Windows app)

  • No telemetry, no accounts, no analytics. NOBAD doesn't run VPN servers. It connects to the servers in the WireGuard configs you import, so your VPN provider's own privacy policy applies to your VPN traffic.
  • Everything stays on your PC: configs and private keys, settings, logs, usage history and diagnostic history, in C:\ProgramData\NobadVPN (readable only by administrators).

02When the app connects

The app connects to other services only in these cases:

WhenWhat is sent, and to whom
You connectYour VPN traffic goes to your VPN server, as set in your config.
Every start (connectivity check)A ping to 1.1.1.1 and one DNS lookup, to tell you whether the internet works.
Look up my exit IP address (off by default)Your public IP address and your servers' addresses, to ipinfo.io (with fallbacks), to show locations on the map.
You run a speed test or service checkDownloads from Cloudflare, and simple requests to the streaming sites being checked.
You use Where to watchYour search and your own TMDB key, to TMDB (titles and JustWatch availability data); look-ups to Wikipedia and Wikidata for documented restrictions.
You use Network IntelligenceDNS queries and test connections to the site you investigate, and to public DNS resolvers.
WireGuard is missingThe official installer is downloaded from download.wireguard.com (only if the bundled copy can't be used).
UpdatesOnly if an update address is configured: the update manifest and installer are downloaded from it.
Crash reportsOff. Nothing is sent. You can export a diagnostics file with private keys removed and attach it to a GitHub issue yourself.

The full technical list is in SYSTEM-INTEGRATION.md.

03This website

  • It is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes the technical data any website request involves (such as your IP address) to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when you open them.
  • No cookies, analytics or advertising trackers are used by NOBAD.
  • Downloads are served by GitHub Releases (GitHub's privacy statement applies).

04Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub, or report something sensitive privately via private vulnerability reporting.
