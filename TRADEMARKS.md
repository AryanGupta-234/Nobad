# NOBAD trademark and brand policy

NOBAD's **code** is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE): you may use, copy, change, fork and redistribute it.
The **NOBAD name and brand** are not part of that licence. This policy explains how you may use them, so that people
always know which builds come from NOBAD.

"The NOBAD brand" means the names **NOBAD**, **NOBAD VPN**, **NOBAD Find**, **NOBAD File Bridge**, the motto
"Software with a point of view.", the NOBAD logos and marks, and anything confusingly similar.

## Always fine, no permission needed
- Saying what's true: "based on NOBAD VPN", "a fork of NOBAD Find", "compatible with NOBAD", "I contributed to NOBAD".
- Writing about NOBAD: articles, reviews, videos, tutorials, talks.
- Forking the repositories on GitHub, building them for yourself and sending changes back as pull requests.
- Linking to the official website or repositories.

## Needs a different name
If you **publish or distribute your own build** (modified or not) outside a pull request to NOBAD:
- give it your own name and logo, not "NOBAD" or anything that looks like it;
- don't use the NOBAD logos, icons or website design;
- don't suggest that NOBAD made, checked or endorses it;
- you may say it's "based on NOBAD" with a link to the original.

Change the product name, window titles, icons, installer and update channel before you ship. The update checks,
download pages and signatures belong to NOBAD's official builds.

## Official builds
Only builds published by the official NOBAD release workflows, from the repositories of the
[`nobad-studio`](https://github.com/nobad-studio) organization on GitHub, are "NOBAD VPN", "NOBAD Find" or
"NOBAD File Bridge". They come with a SHA-256 checksum and a build-provenance attestation you can verify.

## Ask first
For anything else (merchandise, a commercial service, a partnership, using the logo), write to
ludmj99@gmail.com. Permission is usually easy to get for community use.

The NOBAD brand belongs to Aryan Gupta, and to any company he forms for NOBAD. This policy may be updated; the
version in this repository is the current one.
